## Slide 6 · Learning Outcomes — BPM&EA ／ 学习成果：BPM&EA

**EN**

- **L1** — Explain and evaluate the architecture of contemporary applications
- **L2** — Evaluate designs in terms of form, context, ensemble, multiple form-context boundaries and fitness
- **L3** — Analyse and model information systems and applications effectively using patterns, frameworks and constraints

**中文**

- **L1** —— 解释并评估当代应用的架构
- **L2** —— 从形式（form）、语境（context）、整体（ensemble）、多重形式-语境边界以及适配性（fitness）等角度评估设计
- **L3** —— 运用模式、框架与约束，有效地分析和建模信息系统与应用

> 术语说明：**form / context / ensemble / fitness** 是 Christopher Alexander 建筑学理论引入软件设计的一组概念——"形式"指解决方案的结构，"语境"指它所处的环境，"整体"指形式与语境共同构成的完整系统，"适配性"指二者契合的程度。

## Slide 7 · Learning Outcomes — AISD ／ 学习成果：AISD

**EN**

- **L1** — Understand the different contexts for Human Computer Interfaces and be able to define a user-centred design that explicitly recognises the user and is Disability Discrimination Act compliant
- **L2** — Create and specify a software design for a medium-size software product using a software requirement specification, an accepted program design methodology and appropriate design notation
- **L3** — Understand and critically evaluate the project management processes and their relationship to systems development method and practice; including stakeholder requirements analysis, planning, estimating and risk
- **L4** — Understand and manage version control, security, legacy and implementation as they relate to the development, delivery and operation of Information Systems
- **L5** — Determine appropriate testing strategies; create, evaluate, and implement test plans; develop and execute test scripts and assess test results
- **L6** — Explain, select and apply the principles, standards and techniques of quality planning and assurance as they relate to systems development and deliverables

**中文**

- **L1** —— 理解人机界面（HCI）所处的不同语境，并能定义一种明确以用户为中心、且符合《残障歧视法》（Disability Discrimination Act）的设计
- **L2** —— 使用软件需求规格说明、公认的程序设计方法与合适的设计记号（design notation），为中等规模软件产品创建并表述软件设计
- **L3** —— 理解并批判性评估项目管理过程及其与系统开发方法和实践的关系，包括利益相关者需求分析、计划、估算与风险
- **L4** —— 理解并管理版本控制、安全性、遗留系统与实施部署，及其与信息系统开发、交付和运行的关系
- **L5** —— 确定合适的测试策略；创建、评估并实施测试计划；开发并执行测试脚本，并评估测试结果
- **L6** —— 解释、选择并应用质量策划与质量保证的原则、标准和技术，及其与系统开发和交付物的关系
