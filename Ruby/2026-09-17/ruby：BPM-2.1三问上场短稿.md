# Task 2.1 三问 · 上场短稿

对着 Completed Word 的 **2.1**。只讲这三个：stakeholders / processes / dependencies。  
每问中文不超过 100 字。斜体英文直接说。

开场一句（可选）  
*Task 2.1. Three things: who is involved, what processes we have, and what depends on what.*

---

## 1. Identify stakeholders

**题目** Identify different stakeholders of the business  
**中文题** 找出这项业务里不同的利益相关者

**中文（89 字）**  
利益相关者就是谁被这条医院路径影响到。患者和 GP 要及时看病。医生决定收不收、治不治。秘书约号、发信，不能当医生。财务管钱，不能决定治疗。外面还有排班、发信、支付。医生嫌记太多，领导要能查，这是冲突。

**英文**  
*Stakeholders means who is involved.*  
*Patients and GPs want care on time.*  
*Doctors decide medical things.*  
*Secretaries check papers and book. They cannot act like a doctor.*  
*Finance decides money, not treatment.*  
*Outside: calendar, letters, payment.*  
*One conflict: doctors want less recording. Managers want a clear record.*

---

## 2. Identify processes

**题目** Identify all the business processes  
**中文题** 找出全部业务流程（队友说的「进程」就是这个）

**中文（96 字）**  
进程就是一条条能单独开始、结束的活，不要画成一条糊线。先讲四条：秘书核材料；医生决定收不收；只有接受后才能约第一次门诊；然后写信，两周内再打电话。治疗、付钱、诊后信都在第一次门诊之后。每条都有触发、负责人和结束状态。

**英文**  
*Process means one piece of work with a start, an owner, and an end.*  
*Not one big messy line.*  
*First: secretaries check papers. They cannot say yes or no.*  
*Second: the doctor decides.*  
*Third: only after yes, we book the first visit.*  
*Fourth: we send a letter. If the visit is in two weeks, we also call.*  
*Treatment and payment come after the first visit.*

---

## 3. Identify dependencies

**题目** Identify the dependencies among the business processes  
**中文题** 找出这些流程之间的依赖（谁先谁后）

**中文（92 字）**  
依赖就是前面没过，后面不能做。秘书核完，才交给医生。医生拒绝，就停，不能约新患。医生要补材料，回到秘书。医生接受，才能约号，再通知病人。没医生授权，行政不能约治疗。外面排班挂了就等待，不准重复约号。

**英文**  
*Dependency means the next step cannot start too early.*  
*Papers first, then the doctor.*  
*If the doctor says no, we stop. We cannot book.*  
*If he wants more papers, we go back to the secretary.*  
*If he says yes, then we book, then we tell the patient.*  
*No doctor OK, no treatment booking.*  
*If the calendar is down, we wait. No second booking.*

---

## 30 秒收口（老师赶时间）

*Three points. Stakeholders: doctors decide care, finance decides money, admin cannot act like a doctor. Processes: check papers, doctor yes or no, then first booking. Dependency: if the doctor says no, we cannot book.*
