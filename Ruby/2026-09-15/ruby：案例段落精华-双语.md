# ruby：案例段落精华（双语 · 口头用）

对照：Hospital Patient Referral, Treatment and Administration System  
用法：先看中文，下面一行英文可直接讲。专有名词尽量换成日常词。

---

## 专有名词（讲的时候用右边）

| 别硬记这个 | 你就说 |
|------------|--------|
| Referral | a doctor or another hospital sends the patient to us |
| Consultant | the specialist doctor who decides |
| Medical Secretaries | admin staff who check papers, not medical decisions |
| Outpatient Bookings | the team that books first clinic visits |
| Treatment / Chemo Bookings | the team that books treatment |
| Call Handling | the phone team |
| CNS | specialist nurses who can give clinical advice |
| Finance | money team; they do not decide treatment |
| Pathway Coordinator | the person who watches delays |
| Clinic Letter | the official letter after a visit |
| Audit trail | who did what, and when |

**一句话**  
病人从转诊进来，到看病、治疗、付钱、写信、打电话。现在信息太散，医院要做系统串起来。医生定医疗，财务定钱，行政只办事，不能互相越权。

*In short: a patient comes in by referral, then visit, treatment, payment, letters and phone calls. Information is messy now. The new system should connect the path. Doctors decide care. Finance decides money. Admin only carries out the work.*

---

## 主流程（可当开场 30 秒）

秘书收材料（第3段）→ 医生决定收不收（第4段）→ 才能约第一次门诊（第4–5段）→ 看病后医生授权才能约治疗（第7–8段）→ 先分清谁出钱、必要时外部支付（第9–13段）→ 诊后写信（第19–23段）、接线分流（第16–18段）→ 全程留痕（第26–27段）。

*Secretary checks papers → doctor accepts or not → only then book the first visit → after the visit, doctor must authorise treatment → check who pays, use outside payment if needed → write the clinic letter and route phone calls → keep a full record. Doctors decide care, finance decides money, admin only does the admin work.*

---

## 逐段精华

**标题**  
医院患者转诊、治疗与行政系统。  
*A hospital system for referrals, treatment and admin.*

**第1段**  
专科医院管病人从转诊到就诊、诊断、治疗、随访和日常沟通。人很多。看病、办事、管钱必须分开。  
*A specialist hospital looks after patients from referral to follow-up. Many teams. Medical, admin and money work must stay separate.*

**第2段**  
现在靠旧系统、邮件、电话、信和手工本。进度难看、延误难发现、查不清谁做的。所以要上新系统。  
*Today info is in old systems, email, phone, letters and paper. Hard to see progress, delays, or who did what. So they want a new system.*

**第3段**  
新转诊先到秘书。秘书只查材料齐不齐，缺了可以向转诊方要。秘书不能决定收不收。  
*New referrals go to secretaries first. They only check if papers are complete. They cannot accept or reject the patient.*

**第4段**  
材料过了交给医生。医生可以收、拒、再要材料、转到别的专科。要记下原因和是谁定的。只有医生接受后，才能约第一次门诊。  
*Then a doctor reviews it. Accept, reject, ask for more papers, or send to another service. Record the reason and who decided. Book the first visit only after the doctor accepts.*

**第5段**  
接受后，预约组用外面的排班系统找号，再在本院系统确认。一般写信；两周内的还要打电话。  
*After accept, the booking team finds a slot with an outside calendar, then confirms it here. Normally send a letter. If the visit is within two weeks, also phone the patient.*

**第6段**  
每次打电话都要记结果。还要处理：特别急；以及医生要的时间里没号。  
*Log every call: no answer, wrong number, or the patient wants another time. Also handle urgent cases, and when no slot exists in the doctor’s time window.*

**第7段**  
病人来了，医生评估并讲方案。同意后，医生记录同意，并开一张已授权的治疗预约申请。没医生签完，行政不能往下办。  
*At the first visit the doctor assesses the patient. If the patient agrees, the doctor records consent and writes an authorised treatment request. Admin cannot book treatment without that.*

**第8段**  
治疗预约组拿授权单去约。有的用本院，有的靠外面化验、影像等。外面挂了：挂起、通知、重试，不要重复建两个号。  
*The treatment booking team uses that request. Some slots are in-house, some need outside labs or scans. If the outside service is down: keep it pending, tell the team, try again — do not make two bookings.*

**第9段**  
确认治疗前先看钱谁出：医院、保险，还是患者自己。多数人不用自己掏。需要批复时，财务记下机构、批复号、金额、限制。  
*Before confirming treatment, check who pays: hospital, insurer, or the patient. Most patients do not pay themselves. If funding approval is needed, finance records who pays, the reference, amount and limits.*

**第10段**  
要付钱时，本院把金额发给外部支付公司。可网上付或跟财务打电话付。对方回传成功与否和流水。本院不能存完整卡号。  
*If payment is needed, our system sends the amount to an outside payment company. Pay online or by phone with finance. They send back status and a reference. We must not store full card numbers.*

**第11段**  
要先付钱的号：原则上付成功（或有豁免/批复）才能确认。失败可以再付，但不能重复预约，也不能多扣一次钱。  
*If pay-first: do not confirm the booking until payment works, or an exemption is recorded. If payment fails, try again — but no double booking and no double charge.*

**第12段**  
外面扣成功、本院没回执：标成调查，不要自动再扣。病情急可以先治，但必须写原因，事后交财务。  
*If money was taken but we got no confirmation: mark it for check. Do not charge again. If waiting is dangerous, the doctor may treat first, write the reason, then tell finance.*

**第13段**  
付完钱后治疗取消或改了：只有财务能批退款。医生不能批钱；财务不能决定要不要治。整条链要能查。  
*If treatment changes after payment, only finance can approve a refund. Doctors do not approve money. Finance does not decide if treatment is needed. Keep the whole chain traceable.*

**第14段**  
化疗下一周期前可能要复查验血。能不能继续，只有临床能定。  
*Before the next chemo cycle there may be a review and blood test. Only clinical staff decide if treatment continues.*

**第15段**  
改治疗安排必须在系统里提交正式申请。电话邮件口头不算。紧急可先停，事后医生补授权。影响钱的要给财务看。  
*To change a treatment plan, submit a formal request in the system. Email or phone is not enough. In an emergency, stop first, then the doctor signs it later. If money is affected, send it to finance.*

**第16段**  
第一次门诊后，患者可找专科护士问病情，也可打总机。接线组要判断：行政、钱，还是临床。  
*After the first visit, patients can call specialist nurses for clinical questions, or the main phone line. The phone team must sort: admin, money, or clinical.*

**第17段**  
接线员可以答几点在哪。钱的问题不够权限就转财务。不能诊断、不能解读化验、不能建议怎么治。  
*Phone staff may confirm time and place. Money questions go to finance if they cannot answer. They must not diagnose, read test results, or suggest treatment.*

**第18段**  
每个电话都要登记并分流。紧急要马上标出。但“怎样算紧急”医院还没定死，要问老师。  
*Record every enquiry: when, who, which team, what reply, done or not. Mark urgent clinical ones at once. The hospital has not fully defined “urgent” — we should ask the tutor.*

**第19段**  
每次就诊后医生写诊后信。收件人由医生指定：患者、GP、别的医院等。  
*After each visit the doctor writes a clinic letter. The doctor chooses who gets it: patient, GP, or another hospital.*

**第20段**  
信必须医生批准才能发。秘书只能改格式，不能改意思。怀疑写错了退回医生。  
*The doctor must approve the letter before it is sent. Secretaries may fix format, not meaning. If the clinical text looks wrong, send it back.*

**第21段**  
信应在就诊后 7 天内写完并发出。系统要记下各个时间点。  
*Finish and send the letter within seven days of the visit. Record the key dates: visit, write, approve, send.*

**第22段**  
超 7 天 = 延迟。每周催医生。超 1 个月升经理；超 3 个月再往上。  
*After seven days it is late. Weekly reminders to the doctor. After one month, escalate to the admin manager. After three months, higher management.*

**第23段**  
信写完了就别再催。可以填延迟原因。催办记录都要留。  
*Stop reminders after the letter is done. Staff may record a valid reason for delay. Keep the history.*

**第24段**  
医生可申请随访，写明多久内再看。约不到就标出来交给路径组，不能偷偷约到窗外。  
*The doctor may ask for a follow-up in a set time. If no slot in that time, flag it. Do not quietly book outside the window.*

**第25段**  
患者可能取消或没来。要记录。付过钱的问财务退不退。能不能继续治，只有临床能定。  
*Patients may cancel or not attend. Record it. If they already paid, finance decides about the money. Only clinicians decide if treatment continues or stops.*

**第26段**  
病历和钱都敏感。每人只能看自己岗位该看的。关键动作都要记。  
*The system holds private health and money data. Staff only see what their job needs. Log important actions.*

**第27段**  
审计要有谁、何时、做了啥，普通人改不了。还要防止配错病人。系统挂了，线下做的事事后要能补录。  
*Audit logs: who, when, what. Normal users cannot edit them. Do not mix up patients. If the system is down, there must be a way to record work later.*

**第28段**  
管理层要报表看积压和延误。医生嫌记太多耽误看病。这是冲突。  
*Managers want reports on delays and unfinished work. Doctors worry that too much recording steals time from patients. These goals conflict.*

**第29段**  
患者要及时通知、要隐私。有人要纸质，有人要电子，有人要翻译或代表。做法现在也不统一。  
*Patients want timely contact and privacy. Some want paper, some email, some need translation or a helper. Teams do not all work the same way today.*

**第30段（对我们）**  
我们要找出谁要什么、冲突在哪、每步谁授权、外部系统怎么接、哪里会失败，并写下需求。案例没写清的，标出来，问老师，写下假设。  
*We are the requirements team. Find stakeholders, conflicts, who can authorise each step, outside services, and likely failures. If the case is unclear, mark it, ask the tutor, and write our assumptions.*
