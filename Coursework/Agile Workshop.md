## Part 1: Understand The Case Study

Read the full case study as a group. Your first task is to understand the business problem before creating technical tasks.

Discuss and record:

1. What organisation or business context is described?
2. Who are the main stakeholders?
3. What is the main business process?
4. What problem is the proposed system trying to solve?
5. What are the key business rules?
6. What exceptions or alternative paths are described?
7. What external systems or organisations are involved?
8. What information must be captured, stored, transferred or validated?

### Task 1: Case Study Summary

Create a short group summary of the case study.

Your summary should include:

- Main business process.
- Main participants.
- Key requirements.
- Important business rules.
- External services or organisations.
- Assumptions made by the group.

## Part 3: Create The Product Backlog

The product backlog is the full list of work required for the project. It should include modelling, implementation, testing, review and evidence tasks.

Create backlog items based on the case study provided.

Each backlog item should include:

1. Backlog ID.
2. Task title.
3. Description.
4. Priority.
5. Related requirement or business rule.
6. Estimate.
7. First owner.
8. Second owner.
9. Dependencies.
10. Acceptance conditions.
11. Evidence required.
12. Status.

## Part 4: Break The Work into Project Tasks

Each backlog item should be broken into manageable tasks. A task should be small enough to track and complete within a sprint.

Avoid large tasks such as:

1. “Do BPMN”
2. “Create system”
3. “Do testing”
4. “Make presentation”

Use specific tasks such as:

1. Identify process participants.
2. Draft main BPMN flow.
3. Add exception path for rejected request.
4. Create Camunda Form for referral submission.
5. Implement external worker for validation.
6. Test normal workflow path.
7. Record feedback response.

## Part 5: Dependency Mapping

For each task, identify:

1. What must be completed first?
2. Which task depends on this task?
3. Who needs to be informed if this task is delayed?
4. What is the backup plan?

## Part 7: Prepare For Standups

Your group must be ready to provide individual standup updates.

Each student should prepare:

1. What I completed.
2. What I will do next.
3. What is blocking me.
4. What evidence supports my update.
5. Whether I need second-owner support.
6. Whether any handover is required.

## Part 8: Prepare For Sprint Reviews

At each sprint review, the group must demonstrate the current integrated increment.

You should be ready to show:

1. Completed tasks.
2. Unfinished tasks.
3. Key decisions.
4. Evidence of integration.
5. Testing or validation evidence.
6. Feedback received.
7. Backlog updates.
