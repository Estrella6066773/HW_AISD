## Slide 3 · Assessment Overview ／ 考核概览

**EN**

- There are two assessment tasks
  - Coursework (50%)
  - Presentation (50%)
- One case study over 2 units
- This is a Group Based assessment
  - Groups of 5 students
- All members must participate in developing the coursework and in presentation
- Medium of communication / presentation is **English**
- Marks are based on the **individual contribution**

**中文**

  - 课程作业（占 50%）

  - 每组 5 名学生
- 所有成员都必须参与作业撰写与汇报演示

## Slide 5 · Coursework (Part 1) ／ 课程作业（第 1 部分）

**EN**

**Task: Strategic BPMN process models (LO1, LO3)**

Requirements:
- Create high-level BPMN models for the initial system release, showing the wider business process, key organisational roles, responsibilities, and external system interactions.
- Show how the strategic business activities connect to the detailed operational BPMN model that will be implemented.
- Explain the abstraction level used, including what is covered in the model and any gaps between the full business process and the implemented solution.

**中文**

**任务：战略性 BPMN 流程模型（对应学习成果 LO1、LO3）**

要求：
- 为首次系统发布（initial system release）创建高层级 BPMN 模型，展现更宏观的业务流程、关键组织角色、职责，以及与外部系统的交互。
- 说明战略性业务活动如何与将要实现的详细操作性 BPMN 模型相衔接。
- 解释所采用的抽象层级，包括模型中涵盖了哪些内容，以及完整业务流程与实际实现方案之间存在哪些缺口（gaps）。

> **抽象层级**是本任务的关键得分点：高层战略模型不画细节，只表达"哪些活动、谁负责、与什么外部系统交互"；要明确说清你砍掉了什么、为什么砍。

## Slide 6 · Coursework (Part 2) ／ 课程作业（第 2 部分）

**EN**

**Task 02: Socio-technical modelling using i\* (LO2, LO3)**

Requirements:
- Develop i\* SD and SR models for the initial system release.
- Identify key actors, goals, soft goals, tasks, resources and dependencies.
- Explain modelling choices and assumptions, ensuring consistency between the SD and SR models.

**中文**

**任务 02：使用 i\* 进行社会技术建模（LO2、LO3）**

要求：
- 为首次系统发布开发 i\* 的 **SD 模型**（Strategic Dependency，战略依赖模型）与 **SR 模型**（Strategic Rationale，战略理由模型）。
- 识别关键参与者（actors）、目标（goals）、软目标（soft goals）、任务（tasks）、资源（resources）和依赖关系（dependencies）。
- 解释建模选择与假设，并确保 SD 模型与 SR 模型之间保持一致。

> i\* 是一种社会技术建模语言：SD 图只画"谁依赖谁要什么"，SR 图则展开每个参与者内部的理由结构（目标如何分解成任务、软目标如何被满足）。两个模型必须能互相印证。

## Slide 7 · Coursework (Part 3) ／ 课程作业（第 3 部分）

**EN**

**Task 03: Evaluation of alignment with requirements (LO1, LO2, LO3)**

Requirements:
- Evaluate how well the initial release addresses stakeholder goals, dependencies and business requirements.
- Trace selected requirements across the strategic process model, operational BPMN model and implemented solution.
- Identify supported, partially supported and unsupported requirements, and justify improvements for the second release.

**中文**

**任务 03：评估与需求的对齐程度（LO1、LO2、LO3）**

要求：
- 评估首次发布在多大程度上满足了利益相关者目标、依赖关系和业务需求。
- 将选定需求在战略流程模型、操作性 BPMN 模型和实际实现方案之间进行追踪（traceability）。
- 区分出「已支持」「部分支持」「未支持」的需求，并为第二次发布提出改进方案并给出理由。

## Slide 8 · Coursework (Part 4) ／ 课程作业（第 4 部分）

**EN**

**Task 04: Evaluation of acceptance criteria (LO2)**

Requirements:
- Critically evaluate the clarity, measurability, relevance and coverage of the initial release acceptance criteria.
- Execute acceptance tests against a clearly identified version of the solution and report results with supporting evidence.
- Explain unmet criteria, limitations and justified revisions without hiding failures against the original criteria.

**中文**

**任务 04：验收标准的评估（LO2）**

要求：
- 批判性评估首次发布验收标准（acceptance criteria）的清晰性、可度量性、相关性和覆盖度。
- 针对明确标识版本的解决方案执行验收测试，并附证据报告结果。
- 解释未达成的标准、局限性以及有正当理由的修订——**不得隐瞒**相对原始标准的失败项。

> 最后一句是评分导向：诚实报告未通过项并给出合理解释，比假装全部通过得分更高。

## Slide 9 · Coursework — Agile Project Delivery ／ 课程作业：敏捷项目交付

**EN**

**Agile project delivery: Project management and sprint evidence (LO3)**

Requirements:
- Maintain a prioritised product backlog and sprint backlogs with sprint goals, owners, estimates, acceptance conditions, dependencies and status.
- Ensure every activity has a first owner and second owner, with clear progress updates and handover information.
- Keep artefacts, code, notes, testing evidence and retrospective actions in the shared project workspace, using a clear definition of done.

**中文**

**敏捷项目交付：项目管理与冲刺（sprint）证据（LO3）**

要求：
- 维护一份按优先级排序的**产品待办列表（product backlog）**和**冲刺待办列表（sprint backlog）**，其中包含冲刺目标、负责人、估算、验收条件、依赖关系和状态。
- 确保每项活动都有**第一负责人**和**第二负责人**，并有清晰的进度更新与交接信息。
- 将交付物、代码、笔记、测试证据和回顾（retrospective）行动项保存在共享项目工作区中，并采用明确的**完成定义（Definition of Done）**。

> 「第一/第二负责人」是这门课反复强调的要求：任何人失联时，第二负责人能接手。standup 上会被问到。

## Slide 10 · Marked Standups ／ 计分的站会

**EN**

- Two marked standups will assess individual progress, not attendance alone.
- Each student must give a concise update on:
  - Completed work
  - Next actions
  - Blockers or risks
  - Dependencies and ownership
- Students must show supporting evidence from the task board, repository, models or project workspace.
- Students should explain progress towards the sprint goal and identify any second-owner support or handover required.
- Marks are based on the quality, accuracy and evidence of the update, including how issues are identified and addressed.

**中文**

- 有两次**计分的站会（standup）**，考核的是个人进度，而不仅仅是出勤。
- 每位学生必须简要汇报：
  - 已完成的工作
  - 下一步行动
  - 阻碍或风险
  - 依赖关系与责任归属
- 学生必须出示来自任务板、代码仓库、模型或项目工作区的支撑证据。
- 学生应说明朝冲刺目标的推进情况，并指出需要哪些第二负责人的支持或交接。
- 分数取决于汇报的质量、准确性及证据充分程度，包括问题是如何被识别和解决的。

> 空口说"我在做"不算数——要能当场打开仓库/看板指给老师看。

## Slide 11 · Marked Sprint Reviews ／ 计分的冲刺评审

**EN**

- Two marked sprint reviews will assess the group's integrated project increment against the sprint goal and agreed acceptance conditions.
- The group must demonstrate completed work, clearly distinguish unfinished work and explain key decisions, limitations and feedback received.
- All members must be able to explain their own contribution to the sprint.
- **Sprint Review 3**: draft strategic BPMN and i\* SD/SR models, with initial requirements traceability.
- **Sprint Review 4**: refined strategic and i\* models, alignment evaluation and acceptance test evidence.

**中文**

- 有两次**计分的冲刺评审（sprint review）**，依据冲刺目标与约定的验收条件，评估小组整合后的项目增量。
- 小组必须演示已完成的工作，明确区分尚未完成的部分，并解释关键决策、局限性和收到的反馈。
- 所有成员都必须能够说明自己在该冲刺中的贡献。
- **冲刺评审 3**：战略 BPMN 草稿与 i\* SD/SR 模型草稿，附初步的需求追踪。
- **冲刺评审 4**：打磨后的战略模型与 i\* 模型、对齐性评估，以及验收测试证据。

## Slide 26 · Contribution & Individual Mark ／ 贡献度与个人分数

**EN**

Assume a group receives:
- Portfolio group mark – 70%
- Sprint Review 1 – 68%
- Sprint Review 2 – 70%

**Student A:**
- Standup 1 individual mark: 75%
