## Slide 4 · Why Agile Emerged ／ 敏捷为何出现

**EN**

- Traditional software development often assumed that requirements could be fully understood at the start.
- In many real projects, requirements change as users, developers and stakeholders learn more.
- Agile emerged as a response to long development cycles, late feedback and poor alignment with user needs.
- Agile methods prioritise working software, collaboration, adaptation and frequent feedback.

**中文**

- 传统软件开发往往假设需求在项目开始时就能被完全理解。
- 但在许多真实项目中，随着用户、开发者和利益相关者认知的加深，需求会不断变化。
- 敏捷的出现，正是对**开发周期过长、反馈太晚、与用户需求脱节**这些问题的回应。
- 敏捷方法优先考虑：可运行的软件、协作、适应变化和频繁反馈。

Agile projects usually involve:

- Frequent delivery of usable increments.

**中文**

敏捷项目通常包含：

- 通过测试、评审和集成保证技术质量。

这些原则应当体现在你们小组管理考核项目的方式中。

## Slide 6 · What Scrum Is ／ Scrum 是什么

**EN**

- Scrum is a lightweight framework for managing complex work.
- It does not prescribe every technical method.
- Instead, it provides roles, events and artefacts that help teams inspect progress and adapt their plans.
- Scrum works well when the team cannot predict everything at the start and must learn through delivery.

**中文**

- Scrum 是一个用于管理复杂工作的**轻量级框架**。
- 它并不规定具体的技术方法。
- 它提供的是角色、事件和交付物，帮助团队检视进度并调整计划。
- 当团队无法在开始时预测一切、必须通过交付来学习时，Scrum 尤其适用。

## Slide 7 · Empiricism in Scrum ／ Scrum 中的经验主义

**EN**

- Scrum is based on **empiricism**. This means decisions are made using evidence from what has actually happened.
- Scrum depends on three pillars:
  - **Transparency**: work, progress and problems are visible.
  - **Inspection**: the team regularly checks progress and quality.
  - **Adaptation**: the team changes its plan when evidence shows that change is needed.
- Your task board, repository, sprint reviews and testing records all support these three pillars.

**中文**

- Scrum 建立在**经验主义（empiricism）**之上：决策要基于实际发生的事实证据，而不是主观猜测。
- Scrum 依赖三大支柱：
  - **透明（Transparency）**：工作、进度和问题都是可见的。
  - **检视（Inspection）**：团队定期检查进度与质量。
  - **调整（Adaptation）**：当证据表明需要改变时，团队就修改计划。
- 你们的任务看板、代码仓库、冲刺评审和测试记录，都是在支撑这三大支柱。

## Slide 8 · Scrum Theory and Your Assessment ／ Scrum 理论与你们的考核

**EN**

- **Transparency** means tutors and team members can see who is doing what.
- **Inspection** happens during standups, sprint reviews and feedback sessions.
- **Adaptation** happens when the team updates the backlog, changes priorities or improves the second release.
- The assessment rewards evidence that the group has planned, delivered and improved the project over time.

**中文**

- **透明**意味着导师和组员都能看到谁在做什么。
- **检视**发生在站会、冲刺评审和反馈环节中。
- **调整**发生在团队更新待办列表、改变优先级或改进第二次发布的时候。
- 考核奖励的是**能够证明小组随着时间推移完成计划、交付并持续改进**的证据。

## Slide 9 · Scrum Roles ／ Scrum 角色

**EN**

Scrum normally includes:

- **Product Owner**: represents priorities and value.
- **Scrum Master**: supports the process and removes obstacles.
- **Developers**: produce the project increment.

In your assessment, these roles are adapted. The tutor acts partly as the client or reviewer, and the group acts as the development team. The group may nominate a coordinator, but responsibility remains shared.

**中文**

Scrum 通常包含：

- **产品负责人（Product Owner）**：代表优先级与价值。
- **Scrum Master**：支持流程运转、扫除障碍。
- **开发者（Developers）**：产出项目增量。

在你们的考核中，这些角色做了调整：导师部分扮演客户或评审者的角色，小组则扮演开发团队。小组可以推选一名协调人，但责任仍然是**共担**的。

- The development team is responsible for turning backlog items into a working increment.
- In your project, the team includes all group members.
- Each student must contribute to planning, modelling, development, testing, review and evidence.

**中文**

## Slide 11 · First Owner and Second Owner ／ 第一负责人与第二负责人

**EN**

- Each task must have a first owner and a second owner.
- The first owner leads the task and is responsible for delivery.
- The second owner stays informed and can continue the work if needed.
- This supports continuity, reduces delivery risk and avoids single points of failure.
- This also reflects professional practice, where teams manage risk through shared understanding.

**中文**

- 每项任务都必须有第一负责人和第二负责人。
- 第一负责人主导该任务，并对交付负责。
- 第二负责人保持知情，必要时能接手继续推进。
- 这保证了工作的连续性，降低交付风险，避免出现"单点故障"。
- 这也符合职业实践——专业团队通过共享理解来管理风险。

## Slide 12 · Scrum Artefacts ／ Scrum 交付物

**EN**

Scrum uses three main artefacts:

- **Product backlog**: the full list of work needed for the product.
- **Sprint backlog**: the selected work for the current sprint.
- **Increment**: the completed work produced by the end of the sprint.

For your assessment, these artefacts should be visible through your task board, repository, models, forms, workers, tests and documentation.

**中文**

Scrum 使用三种主要交付物（artefacts）：

- **产品待办列表（Product backlog）**：产品所需全部工作的完整清单。
- **冲刺待办列表（Sprint backlog）**：本冲刺中被选中的那部分工作。
- **增量（Increment）**：冲刺结束时产出的已完成工作。

在你们的考核中，这些交付物应当能通过任务看板、代码仓库、模型、表单、工作器、测试和文档被看到。

## Slide 13 · Product Backlog ／ 产品待办列表

**EN**

- The product backlog contains all known requirements, tasks and improvements.
- It should include modelling, implementation, testing, documentation and evidence tasks.
- Items should be prioritised according to value, risk and dependency.
- The backlog should change when the team receives feedback or identifies new work.
- A good backlog helps the team decide what to do next and why.

**中文**

- 产品待办列表包含所有已知的需求、任务和改进项。
- 它应当涵盖建模、实现、测试、文档和举证类任务。
- 待办项应依据**价值、风险和依赖关系**排定优先级。
- 当团队收到反馈或识别出新工作时，待办列表应当随之更新。
- 好的待办列表能帮助团队决定接下来做什么、以及为什么这么做。

---

## Slide 14 · Backlog Item Quality ／ 待办项的质量标准

**EN**

A useful backlog item should include:

- A clear description of the work.
- The reason the work matters.
- Priority.
- Estimate.
- First owner and second owner.
- Dependencies.
- Acceptance conditions.
- Current status.
- Evidence link or location.

Without these details, the task becomes difficult to manage and assess.

**中文**

一条有用的待办项应包含：

- 对工作的清晰描述。
- 这项工作为何重要。
- 优先级。
- 估算（工作量/时间）。
- 第一负责人与第二负责人。
- 依赖关系。
- 验收条件。
- 当前状态。
- 证据的链接或存放位置。

缺少这些信息，任务就会变得难以管理和评估。

> 这 9 条基本就是老师检查看板时逐条对照的清单，照着建任务卡最稳。

## Slide 15 · Sprint Planning ／ 冲刺计划

**EN**

- Sprint planning happens at the start of each sprint.
- The team agrees the sprint goal and selects a manageable sprint backlog.
- The selected work should be realistic for the available time.
- The team should consider dependencies, risks and assessment deadlines.
- Sprint planning should end with a clear answer to: "What will we deliver by the end of this sprint?"

**中文**

- 冲刺计划在每个冲刺开始时进行。
- 团队共同确认**冲刺目标**，并挑选出可驾驭的冲刺待办列表。
- 所选工作相对于可用时间应当是现实的。
- 团队应当考虑依赖关系、风险以及考核截止时间。
- 冲刺计划结束时，应当能明确回答："本冲刺结束时我们会交付什么？"

---

## Slide 16 · Sprint Goal ／ 冲刺目标

**EN**

- The sprint goal gives the sprint a clear purpose.
- It is more than a list of tasks.
- It explains what useful outcome the team aims to achieve.
- Example: "Produce an executable BPMN model with the main workflow and one exception path ready for review."
- A clear sprint goal helps the group make decisions when time is limited.

**中文**

- 冲刺目标为这次冲刺赋予明确的目的。
- 它不只是一串任务清单。
- 它说明团队要达成什么**有用的成果**。
- 示例："产出一个可执行的 BPMN 模型，包含主流程和一条异常路径，可供评审。"
- 时间紧张时，清晰的冲刺目标能帮助小组做取舍决策。

> 注意示例的写法：动词 + 可验收的产物 + 范围界定（只有主流程和一条异常路径）。

---

## Slide 17 · Definition of Done ／ 完成定义

**EN**

- The Definition of Done describes what must be true before work can be considered complete.
- It protects the team from treating unfinished work as finished.
- A task may be "in progress" even if a first draft exists.
- In your project, "done" should include review, integration and evidence.
- This is important because unreviewed or unintegrated work creates hidden risk.

**中文**

- 完成定义（Definition of Done, DoD）描述了在工作可被视为完成之前，必须满足哪些条件。
- 它防止团队把未完成的工作当成已完成。
- 即使已经写出初稿，任务也可能仍然处于"进行中"。
- 在你们的项目中，"完成"应当包含评审、集成和举证。
- 这一点很重要，因为未经评审或未集成的工作会带来隐性风险。

---

## Slide 18 · Example Definition of Done ／ 完成定义示例

**EN**

A project task is done when:

- It meets the agreed acceptance conditions.
- Another team member has reviewed it.
- It is stored in the shared repository or workspace.
- It is linked to evidence.
- It works with the wider project increment.
- Any limitations are recorded.
- The team can demonstrate it in a sprint review.

**中文**

项目任务满足以下条件才算完成：

- 满足约定的验收条件。
- 已由另一位组员评审过。
- 已存入共享仓库或工作区。
- 已关联到证据。
- 能与更宏观的项目增量协同工作。
- 任何局限都已被记录下来。
- 团队能够在冲刺评审中演示它。

## Slide 19 · Scrum Events ／ Scrum 事件

**EN**

Scrum uses events to create regular points for planning, inspection and adaptation.

The main events are:

- Sprint.
- Sprint planning.
- Daily Scrum or standup.
- Sprint review.
- Sprint retrospective.

In your assessment, standups and sprint reviews are also marked activities.

**中文**

Scrum 通过一系列事件，为计划、检视和调整建立固定的时间节点。

主要事件包括：

- 冲刺（Sprint）。
- 冲刺计划会（Sprint planning）。
- 每日 Scrum 或站会（Daily Scrum / standup）。
- 冲刺评审（Sprint review）。
- 冲刺回顾（Sprint retrospective）。

在你们的考核中，站会和冲刺评审同时也是**计分活动**。

## Slide 20 · Standups ／ 站会

**EN**

- A standup is a short meeting focused on progress and blockers.
- It is not a long technical discussion.
- Each member should explain completed work, next actions and blockers.
- The update should be supported by evidence.
- Standups help the team identify risks early and coordinate work across tasks.

**中文**

- 站会是一个短会，聚焦于进度和阻碍。
- 它不是冗长的技术讨论。
- 每位成员应说明已完成的工作、下一步行动和阻碍。
- 汇报应当有证据支撑。
- 站会帮助团队尽早发现风险，并在各项任务之间做好协调。

## Slide 21 · Sprint Reviews ／ 冲刺评审

**EN**

- A sprint review examines the increment produced during the sprint.
- The group demonstrates completed work against the sprint goal.
- The team explains unfinished work, decisions, feedback and limitations.
- The review should lead to backlog updates.
- A sprint review is about the product increment, not just a presentation.

**中文**

- 冲刺评审检视本冲刺产出的增量。
- 小组对照冲刺目标演示已完成的工作。
- 团队要说明未完成的部分、所做的决策、收到的反馈以及局限。
- 评审应当带来待办列表的更新。
- 冲刺评审的核心是**产品增量**，而不只是一场演讲展示。

---

## Slide 22 · Sprint Retrospective ／ 冲刺回顾

**EN**

- A retrospective focuses on **how** the team worked.
- It is different from a sprint review, which focuses on the product increment.
- The team should discuss what worked well, what caused problems and what should change in the next sprint.
- Retrospectives support continuous improvement.
- Your group should record improvement actions after each review.

**中文**

- 回顾会聚焦于团队**如何工作**（过程本身）。
- 这与冲刺评审不同——后者关注的是产品增量。
- 团队应当讨论：哪些做得好、哪些导致了问题、下个冲刺应当改变什么。
- 回顾会支撑持续改进。
- 你们小组应当在每次评审后记录下改进措施。

> 一句话区分：**Review 看产品，Retro 看过程。**

## Slide 23 · Incremental Delivery ／ 增量交付

**EN**

- Scrum encourages incremental delivery.
- Each sprint should produce something that can be inspected.
- In software engineering, an increment may be a model, workflow, form, service task, test plan or working feature.
- The increment should become more complete over time.
- This reduces the risk of discovering major problems only at the final deadline.

**中文**

- Scrum 鼓励增量交付。
- 每个冲刺都应当产出**可被检视**的东西。
- 在软件工程中，增量可以是一个模型、一条工作流、一个表单、一个服务任务、一份测试计划，或一个可运行的功能。
- 增量应当随着时间推移越来越完整。
- 这能降低"直到最后截止日期才发现重大问题"的风险。

## Slide 24 · Common Problems in Student Scrum Projects ／ 学生 Scrum 项目中的常见问题

**EN**

- The task board exists but is not updated.
- Tasks are too large or too vague.
- Work is assigned to one person without second-owner support.
- Models, code and tests are developed separately and integrated too late.
- Standup updates describe effort rather than evidence.
- Sprint reviews show slides but not the working increment.
- Feedback is received but not added to the backlog.

**中文**

- 任务看板建了，但从不更新。
- 任务颗粒度过大或过于含糊。
- 工作只压给一个人，没有第二负责人支持。
- 模型、代码和测试各自为政，集成得太晚。
- 站会汇报只讲"我很努力"，而不是拿出证据。
- 冲刺评审只放幻灯片，不展示能跑的增量。
- 收到了反馈，却没有写进待办列表。

> 这一页几乎就是扣分清单，反向对照检查即可。

## Slide 25 · What Good Scrum Looks Like ／ 好的 Scrum 是什么样

**EN**

- The team has a clear sprint goal.
- The backlog is prioritised and realistic.
- Tasks have first and second owners.
- Progress is visible before the deadline.
- Evidence is stored in the shared workspace.
- The increment can be demonstrated.
- Feedback leads to backlog updates.
- The team improves its process after each sprint.

**中文**

- 团队有清晰的冲刺目标。
- 待办列表有优先级排序，且切合实际。
- 每项任务都有第一负责人和第二负责人。
- 在截止日期之前，进度就是可见的。
- 证据存放在共享工作区中。
- 增量是可以当场演示的。
- 反馈会转化为待办列表的更新。
- 团队在每个冲刺后都会改进自己的流程。
