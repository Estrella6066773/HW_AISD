**Two kinds of work, kept apart.** This plan holds both, in two separate tables.

They are the graded coursework artefacts: the feedback response record, the standup records, the sprint-review records, the plan evaluation, the presentation deck, the contribution record and the Definition of Done sign-off. Part B replaces the former `PB-33`, `PB-35`, `PB-36`, `PB-37`, `PB-17` and `PB-29`, which were withdrawn on 2026-10-16 (see Note 9 of the backlog).

## What this plan deliberately does not include

- No new case scope. The second release is marked on improving and verifying the solution, not on adding case functions that were never in the first release.
- No claim that any unreceived feedback has been actioned.
- No `PB` number on any row in part B. Coursework process work is not product scope, and giving it a backlog number is what made the backlog carry two different kinds of thing at once.
