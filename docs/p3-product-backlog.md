## Notes

1. **Numbering**: PB-01 to PB-15 follow the numbering already used in the Part 4 task breakdown and are not renumbered; new items are added from PB-16 onwards. The table is ordered by priority in three blocks, so gaps in the numbering are expected.
2. **Estimates are first-owner hours**, on the same basis as Part 4 and Part 6. A backlog item's hours equal the sum of its task cards.
3. **The Evidence column states the material required.** The actual files are delivered to the shared area by the relevant date, named by item ID.
4. **P0 and sprints**: P0 is the scope to be closed in the initial release (28 September). Sprint 1 covers specification and planning only, pulling from P0 the items directly related to referral check → consultant decision → first clinic booking, namely PB-01 to PB-04, plus PB-15 for standup and review preparation — five items in all; the remaining P0 items are taken up in later sprints. The authoritative scope for this sprint is the Sprint Goal in `docs/p6-sprint-backlog.md`.
5. **Link to task cards**: PB-01 to PB-07 map to T01 to T11 in Part 4, and PB-08 to PB-15 map to T12 to T20; the mapping is in Part 4 (`docs/p4-work-breakdown.md`).
6. **To be confirmed**: how urgency is determined for enquiries. Case para 18 states that the hospital has not yet agreed a complete set of rules for this. No automatic grading is done in this release: any enquiry involving symptoms, treatment side effects or medication is routed to clinical staff and marked urgent, while the grading rules are marked as to be confirmed and will be raised with the tutor. External scheduling and payment interfaces are mocked in this release, and the report must state those boundaries.
7. **Open decision**: whether the audit item (PB-11) belongs to the initial release or the second. Part 6 places it in the second, but among the actions listed in case para 26 the referral decision and the treatment authorisation are core, so the recommendation is to bring at least those two into the initial release.
8. **Status values**: In progress means work has started, Not started means work has not started, and an item may be marked complete only when there is both an openable output and a recorded evidence path; items without evidence are not marked complete. As of 16 September 2026 the items in progress are PB-01 (permission rules and the main path written into the Part 1 case summary), PB-15 (Product Backlog and Sprint Backlog uploaded and all four members joined), PB-17 (contribution record being assembled), PB-28 (traceability matrix drafted) and PB-29 (Definition of Done drafted); the other twenty-four have no output yet and are recorded as Not started. This release covers specification and planning only, so no item reaches complete.
