## Notes

1. **Numbering**: PB-01 to PB-15 follow the numbering already used in the Part 4 task breakdown and are not renumbered; new items are added from PB-16 onwards, and second-release items continue from PB-30. The tables are ordered by priority, so gaps in the numbering are expected. PB-15, PB-17, PB-29, PB-32, PB-33, PB-35, PB-36, PB-37 and PB-38 are gaps because those items were withdrawn on 2026-10-16 (Note 9); withdrawn numbers are not reused. **This file is the single authoritative numbering.** `Est/est：part3产品待办.md` was written on 15 September with an earlier numbering and differs from PB-16 onwards; it is kept as an archived draft with a conversion table at its head, so an ID cited from PB-16 upwards always means this file and never the Chinese table.
2. **Estimates are first-owner hours**, on the same basis as Part 4 and Part 6. A backlog item's hours equal the sum of its task cards.
3. **The Evidence column states the material required.** The actual files are delivered to the shared area by the relevant date, named by item ID.
4. **P0 and sprints**: P0 is the scope to be closed in the initial release (28 September). Sprint 1 covers specification and planning only, pulling from P0 the items directly related to referral check → consultant decision → first clinic booking, namely PB-01 to PB-04. The standup and Sprint Review preparation that ran alongside it is process work recorded in the Part 7 table and the Part 8 record, not a backlog item; it was PB-15 until 16 October. The authoritative scope for that sprint is the Sprint Goal in `docs/p6-sprint-backlog.md`.
5. **Link to task cards**: PB-01 to PB-07 map to T01 to T11 in Part 4, and PB-08 to PB-14 map to T12 to T19; the mapping is in Part 4 (`docs/p4-work-breakdown.md`). Part 4 also carries T20, standup and Sprint Review preparation; T20 remains a task card, but its former backlog ID PB-15 is withdrawn, so T20 is now tracked against the Part 7 and Part 8 records.
6. **To be confirmed**: how urgency is determined for enquiries. Case para 18 states that the hospital has not yet agreed a complete set of rules for this. No automatic grading is done in this release: any enquiry involving symptoms, treatment side effects or medication is routed to clinical staff and marked urgent, while the grading rules are marked as to be confirmed and will be raised with the tutor. External scheduling and payment interfaces are mocked in this release, and the report must state those boundaries.
7. **Open decision**: whether the audit item (PB-11) belongs to the initial release or the second. Part 6 places it in the second, but among the actions listed in case para 26 the referral decision and the treatment authorisation are core, so the recommendation is to bring at least those two into the initial release.
8. **Status values**: In progress means work has started, Not started means work has not started, and an item may be marked complete only when there is both an openable output and a recorded evidence path; items without evidence are not marked complete. As of 16 September 2026 the items in this table that were in progress were PB-01 (permission rules and the main path written into the Part 1 case summary) and PB-28 (traceability matrix drafted); the rest had no output yet. Three further items were in progress on that date — PB-15, PB-17 and PB-29 — and have since been withdrawn as process work (Note 9). The initial release covered specification and planning only, so no item reached complete.
